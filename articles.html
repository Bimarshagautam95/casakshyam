Compliance & Tax Articles

Estimated Income Tax Installments

According to the Income Tax Act, 2058, businesses must file and pay estimated tax in three installments: Poush Masaan (40%), Chaitra Masaan (70%), and Asar Masaan (100%).

Annual Income Tax Return (Dakhila)

Final annual filings must be submitted within 3 months of fiscal year-end (Ashwin Masaan). A formal extension under Section 98 can grant an extra 3 months to Poush Masaan.

Mandatory VAT Registration Rules

VAT registration is compulsory under the VAT Act, 2052 if trailing 12-month turnover exceeds रू 50 Lakhs for goods or रू 20 Lakhs for services/mixed businesses.

Monthly VAT Filing & Penalty Metrics

VAT returns and payments must be settled within 25 days from the end of each Nepalese calendar month. Delays prompt a statutory fine of रू 1,000 plus interest.

Statutory Audit Records Checklist

Prepare for external financial verification by organizing your Trial Balance, General Ledgers, Bank Reconciliations (BRS), Fixed Asset Registers, and Year-End Stock Sheets.

E-TDS Withholding Management

Withholding agents must deposit taxes withheld from rent, salaries, and consultancy fees, and submit the portal E-TDS details within 25 days of the following month.

Navigating IRD Tax Audits & Scrutiny

When selected for formal assessment by the IRO or Large Taxpayers Office (LTO), structured defenses and legal representations protect your firm against arbitrary exposure.

Administrative Review Procedures

Contest disputed tax assessments by applying to the Director General of the IRD within 30 days, along with the mandatory 100% undisputed and 25% disputed cash deposits.

Annual OCR Regulatory Filings

Under the Companies Act, 2063, Private Limited companies must regularly upload AGM minutes, Audited Financial Statements, and share structure updates to the OCR portal.
