According to the Income Tax Act, 2058, every corporate entity or individual with assessable business income must file an estimated tax return and pay tax in three installments through the IRD portal:
  • First Installment: By Poush Masaan (mid-January) — 40% of the total estimated tax liability.
  • Second Installment: By Chaitra Masaan (mid-April) — 70% of the total estimated tax liability.
  • Third Installment: By Asar Masaan (mid-July) — 100% of the total estimated tax liability.

The final annual Income Tax Return (Dakhila) must be submitted within 3 months from the fiscal year-end closing, which falls on Ashwin Masaan (mid-October). Companies can apply for a formal extension under Section 98 of the Income Tax Act for an additional 3 months, moving the final deadline to Poush Masaan.

Under the Value Added Tax Act, 2052, registration for VAT is compulsory if your business turnover over the past 12 consecutive months exceeds:
  • रू 50 Lakhs (NPR 5 Million) for businesses dealing strictly in goods.
  • रू 20 Lakhs (NPR 2 Million) for businesses dealing in services or a mix of goods and services.

VAT-registered entities must file their returns and pay due taxes within 25 days from the end of each Nepalese calendar month. Missing this deadline triggers an automatic fine of रू 1,000 per tax period alongside interest calculations on the outstanding tax amount.

To prepare for a statutory financial examination by our firm, you will need to organize:
  • Final Trial Balance, General Ledgers, and bank statements with Bank Reconciliation (BRS).
  • Fixed Assets Register and verified Year-End Stock Sheets.
  • Purchase/Sales books, expense vouchers, and TDS verification statements.
  • Company documents: Registration Certificate, MoA (Prabandha Patra), and AoA (Niyamawali).

As a withholding agent under the Income Tax Act, any business making payments for rent, salaries, or consultancy services must withhold TDS at the applicable rate. The deducted tax must be paid, and the corresponding E-TDS return must be submitted on the IRD portal within 25 days of the following Nepalese month.

If your business is selected for a tax audit or assessment by the IRO or Large Taxpayers Office (LTO), CA Sakshyam Gautam & Associates represents you completely. We analyze the legal discrepancies, draft structured technical defenses, and attend departmental hearings to minimize unfair tax exposure.

If you disagree with an amended tax assessment from a Tax Officer, you can apply for an Administrative Review to the Director General of the IRD within 30 days of receiving the order. This requires a mandatory cash deposit of 100% of the undisputed tax amount and 25% of the disputed tax and fines.

Under the Companies Act, 2063, all registered private firms must submit key documents to the OCR portal annually to avoid penalties:
  • Annual General Meeting (AGM) attendance and minutes (within 30 days of the meeting).
  • Audited Financial Statements approved by the Board and Shareholders.
  • Updated Share Register and structural changes in company ownership, if any.
